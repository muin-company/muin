## What 48 Hours Taught Us

1. **Sprints are about priorities, not focus.**  
   We didn't make 7 Gallery shots—we completed 4. What mattered was quality, not quantity.

2. **Explode blockers early.**  
   PH account and logo should've been resolved at D-9. Blockers pushed to D-8 cascade delays into D-7, D-6.

3. **70% progress ≠ 70% done**  
   The last 30% takes 50% of the time. It's not Parkinson's Law—it's the Law of Blockers.
