## 💡 Week 2 Lessons

### "100% Ready" Doesn't Exist
The big lesson from Week 2: **If you wait until it's perfect, you'll never ship.** Launch at 80% and fill the remaining 20% with feedback.

GumsiAI, Factory Dashboard, Family Agents—all shipped with "could polish more..." thoughts abandoned. Result? Real user feedback was more accurate than a week of internal deliberation.

### Marketing Matters as Much as Product
Great product doesn't mean people magically find it. **You have to tell them. Repeatedly, across channels.**

Week 1: 100% building. Week 2: 50-50 (building + prep). Week 3? **70% marketing, 30% improvements.**

### Voice is a Game Changer
After testing voice interfaces for a few days following Josh's proposal: **Text chat and voice conversation are completely different experiences.**

Voice is:
- More natural (speaking is faster than typing)
- More intimate (voice conveys emotion)
- More accessible (while driving, cooking, walking)

When OpenClaw integrates Voice, AI agents evolve from **"tools"** to **"colleagues."**
